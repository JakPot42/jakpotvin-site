[research papers]
- Predicting the adopted organisation before simulating it (Study 2A)
Preregistered November 2026 · Paper in preparation

Earlier work found that chemistries grown from lifeless conditions adopt their
smallest organisation, but that was only known after finding every stable state.
This study asks whether the outcome can be predicted before the full dynamics
are simulated, which is what a law has to do.

Plan: [https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/NAWZD](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/NAWZD)
Amendment: [https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/AGFY9](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/AGFY9)
