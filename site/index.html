Atom by atom

Plate X: in 1669 Hennig Brand boiled down urine and found a substance that glowed in the dark. It was phosphorus, the first element whose discoverer we know by name.

AI is making design nearly free. Making things is becoming the bottleneck.

Atomically precise manufacturing is how the physical world catches up, and it starts with knowing how reliable each atomic step is. I build the open data and tools that measure that.

  • What has been measured so farEvery published atomic step with a count
  • Atom Factory CalculatorHow many good parts a machine could make in a day, from measured rates
  • The bottleneck mapPublished 15 December
  • mechdb, the open datasetOn GitHub, 15 December
